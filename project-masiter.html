Project overview

Masiter is designed to replace scattered paper records with a connected digital workflow for everyday shop and small-business operations. Product and stock information, sales invoices, customer details, purchase history and receivables are organized within one app.

The application is built with Flutter and Dart and is available for Android and the web. It supports Arabic and English, including right-to-left layouts for Arabic, as well as light and dark themes.

This is a collaborative project developed by Kirolos Emad and Rimon Abdelmasih.

Key features

  • Products and inventory: organize products by category and unit, store product images with Supabase Storage, monitor stock and receive low-stock notifications.
  • Sales invoices: create and manage sales invoices, export them as PDF, and prepare print layouts for 58 mm thermal, 80 mm thermal and A4 paper.
  • Customers and purchase history: keep customer details and purchase activity together for easier review.
  • Receivables and debt collection: track customer balances and apply payments to the oldest outstanding invoice first.
  • Reminders: provide low-stock notifications and morning reminders related to outstanding receivables.
  • Business data sharing: share business data with another account through an email-based sharing mechanism.
  • Account access: register and sign in with Firebase Authentication, including email-verification support.
  • Localization and themes: support Arabic and English, RTL layouts, light and dark themes.
  • Responsive experience: adapt the interface for mobile and web, with cached product images and error handling.

Architecture and implementation

The code separates presentation and data responsibilities: BLoC/Cubit manages application and UI state, while GetIt provides dependency injection. GoRouter organizes navigation between screens, and localization resources support English and Arabic interfaces.

Firebase Authentication handles account access, Cloud Firestore provides cloud-backed business data, and Supabase Storage is used for product images. PDF generation and printing libraries support exporting and printing invoices in the intended formats.

The supplied project README identifies the main source areas as the Flutter application under lib/, reusable assets and translations under assets/, screenshot documentation under docs/screenshots/, and Firebase configuration at the project root.

Collaboration and learning

Masiter is a collaborative project with Rimon Abdelmasih. The application brings together several connected business workflows, which makes consistency across products, invoices, customer balances, inventory and shared business data an important part of the overall experience.

The project also brings together cross-platform UI development, state management with BLoC/Cubit, cloud authentication and data, image storage, localization and invoice document generation.
